<h2>Why People Switch From Bolt.new</h2>
<p>Bolt.new&#8217;s pitch is strong: build apps, websites, and prototypes from prompts in the browser. Its official pricing page lists a free plan with public and private projects, a 300K daily token limit, 1M monthly tokens, hosting, databases, and Bolt branding. Pro is $25 per month with no daily token limit, starting at 10M monthly tokens, custom domains, private sharing, larger uploads, and token rollover. Teams is $30 per member per month.</p>
<p>That is generous for quick builds. The catch is that serious app work is rarely straight. You generate, test, fix, change the data model, rewrite a screen, add auth, then ask the model to repair what broke. Tokens disappear fastest when the project is messy.</p>
<p>There is also the environment question. Bolt runs in the browser through StackBlitz&#8217;s WebContainers, which is excellent for many JavaScript apps. It is less comfortable when you need native dependencies, unusual backend services, a long-lived repository workflow, or tight control over deployment infrastructure.</p>

<h2>How to Choose the Right Bolt.new Alternative</h2>
<p>If you want the closest overall switch, start with Lovable. If you want an open-source and local route, test Dyad.
