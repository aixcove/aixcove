<h2>What Is Claude?</h2>
<p>Claude is an advanced AI assistant created by Anthropic, a company focused on AI safety research. Known for its thoughtful, nuanced responses and strong emphasis on helpfulness and harmlessness, Claude has become a top choice for professionals who need reliable AI support for complex tasks. The latest Claude models excel at long-context reasoning, code generation, creative writing, and detailed analysis.</p>
<h2>Key Features and Capabilities</h2>
<p>Claude provides a comprehensive suite of AI-powered features:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Extended context window</strong> — Processes up to 200K tokens, ideal for analyzing long documents and codebases</li>
<li><strong>Advanced coding</strong> — Writes, reviews, and debugs code with strong understanding of software architecture</li>
<li><strong>Visual analysis</strong> — Reads and interprets images, charts, diagrams, and handwritten notes</li>
<li><strong>Safe and accurate outputs</strong> — Trained with Constitutional AI to minimize hallucinations and harmful content</li>
<li><strong>Tool use and APIs</strong> — Integrates with external tools, databases, and workflows via the Anthropic API</li>
<li><strong>Multi-language support</strong> — Communicates fluently in dozens of languages</li>
</ul>
<h2>Pricing Plans</h2>
<p>Claude offers a free tier with daily usage limits. The Pro plan at $20/month provides significantly higher usage, priority access during peak times, and early access to new features. The Team plan at $25/month per user adds collaboration tools and admin controls. Enterprise plans are available for organizations needing custom deployments and advanced security.</p>
<h2>Who Should Use Claude?</h2>
<p>Claude is particularly well-suited for knowledge workers, researchers, writers, and developers who value accuracy and depth. Its long-context capabilities make it excellent for legal document review, academic research, technical documentation, and large-scale code analysis. Organizations concerned with AI safety and responsible deployment often prefer Claude over alternatives.</p>
<h2>Claude vs Other AI Assistants</h2>
<p>Claude differentiates itself from ChatGPT and Gemini through its focus on safety, accuracy, and long-form content quality. While ChatGPT leads in plugin ecosystem breadth and Gemini excels in multimodal integration, Claude consistently ranks highest in user satisfaction for tasks requiring careful reasoning, nuanced writing, and honest acknowledgment of limitations.</p>
