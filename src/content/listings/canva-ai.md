<h2>What Is Canva AI?</h2>
<p>Canva AI is the AI-assisted design layer inside Canva’s editable visual workspace. It includes prompt-based design and media assistance alongside templates, brand assets, collaboration, presentation, document, video, and social-design tools. The key advantage is that an AI-generated starting point remains in a familiar editable layout, where a teammate can replace copy, adjust a brand kit, resize formats, and export.</p>
<p>It is not a full substitute for specialised illustration, motion, or desktop-layout work, but it is a fast way to go from idea to a shareable first draft.</p>
<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Prompt-based design and media generation</strong> inside an editable canvas.</li>
<li><strong>Brand kits and templates</strong> for consistent, on-brand output.</li>
<li><strong>Collaboration</strong> so teammates can edit and comment in place.</li>
<li><strong>Multi-format export</strong> for social, presentation, print, and web.</li>
</ul>
<p>Sketch the structure in a template first, then generate media into that layout so the result is already on-brand. Swap in final copy and check trademarks and rights before sending.</p>
<h2>Pricing</h2>
<p>Canva offers <strong>Free at $0</strong> plus paid Pro, Business, and Enterprise. The official page localises prices by billing country and currency; at verification it showed <strong>Pro at ¥11,800/year for one person</strong> and <strong>Business at ¥18,800/year per person</strong>. Because USD list pricing is region-dependent, use the official selector rather than assuming a conversion. Prices checked August 2026 — see <a href="https://www.canva.com/pricing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Canva pricing</a>.</p>
<h2>Limitations and Trade-offs</h2>
<p>Free and paid plans use shared AI allowances, and higher-tier AI uses consume more of that allowance; exact usage varies by tool. Canva’s help text also notes that offerings evolve. Generated images and copy require the same trademark, factual, and rights review as other AI outputs.</p>
<h2>Who Should Use Canva AI?</h2>
<p>It suits marketing, social, and operations teams who need on-brand assets fast without a designer on call. Teams with strict identity systems or print production will still use dedicated design software.</p>
<h2>Canva AI vs Alternatives</h2>
<p>Use <a href="/listing/midjourney/">Midjourney</a> for high-quality generated images, and Canva AI for turning those into finished, editable brand layouts.</p>
