<h2>What Is Flowise?</h2>
<p>Flowise is an open-source visual builder for LLM applications and AI workflows. It helps teams design chatbots, retrieval pipelines, agent flows, and integrations through a node-based interface that reduces the amount of custom code needed to prototype and ship AI products.</p>
<h2>Key Features and Capabilities</h2>
<p>Flowise provides a practical set of AI capabilities for modern users and teams:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Visual drag-and-drop builder</strong> &#8211; Design LLM apps and AI workflows through a node-based editor</li>
<li><strong>RAG and chatbot support</strong> &#8211; Build document chat, retrieval flows, and conversational assistants faster</li>
<li><strong>Wide model compatibility</strong> &#8211; Connect with OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama, and other providers</li>
<li><strong>Developer acceleration</strong> &#8211; Prototype AI products quickly before hardening them into production systems</li>
<li><strong>Open-source extensibility</strong> &#8211; Customize nodes, integrations, and deployment patterns to fit internal needs</li>
<li><strong>Workflow transparency</strong> &#8211; Makes it easier to inspect how prompts, memory, and tools are connected</li>
</ul>
<h2>Pricing Plans</h2>
<p>Flowise is open-source and free to deploy. Real cost depends on hosting, database usage, and the model providers connected to each workflow.</p>
<h2>Who Should Use Flowise?</h2>
<p>Flowise is a strong fit for startups, internal tooling teams, and developers who want a faster way to prototype chatbots, RAG assistants, and LLM pipelines without writing everything from scratch.</p>
<h2>Flowise vs LangFlow vs Dify</h2>
<p>Flowise is widely used as a practical visual builder for LLM workflows. LangFlow is similar in concept with a close LangChain connection, while Dify offers a more productized application-layer experience. For teams that want open-source control plus visual workflow speed, Flowise is a solid option.</p>
