<h2>What is Gemma?</h2>
<p>Gemma is Google’s family of open-weight AI models designed for developers, researchers, and teams that want capable language models they can run, tune, or integrate outside a closed chatbot product. It fits the broader open model ecosystem alongside tools like Llama, Mistral, and Qwen.</p>
<h2>Best for</h2>
<ul>
<li>Developers testing open-weight model workflows</li>
<li>Teams evaluating local or self-hosted AI options</li>
<li>Researchers comparing compact model performance</li>
<li>Builders who want Google-backed model releases without relying only on hosted Gemini apps</li>
</ul>
<h2>Key strengths</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Open-weight access:</strong> useful for experimentation and deployment flexibility.</li>
<li><strong>Developer-friendly ecosystem:</strong> works with common model tooling and local inference stacks.</li>
<li><strong>Google model lineage:</strong> benefits from Google’s broader AI research direction.</li>
<li><strong>Flexible use cases:</strong> summarization, classification, chat, and lightweight assistant workflows.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Limitations</h2>
<p>Gemma is not the same as the Gemini consumer app. Running open models well still requires hardware planning, evaluation, safety checks, and deployment work. If you want a hosted assistant, compare with <a href="/listing/gemini/">Gemini</a>. If you want local app interfaces, compare with <a href="/listing/open-webui/">Open WebUI</a> and <a href="/listing/ollama/">Ollama</a>.</p>
<h2>FAQ</h2>
<h3>Is Gemma open source?</h3>
<p>Gemma is commonly described as an open-weight model family. Always check the current license terms for allowed commercial and deployment use.</p>
<h3>Should I choose Gemma or Gemini?</h3>
<p>Choose Gemma for open-weight model work. Choose Gemini for a hosted Google AI assistant and API ecosystem.</p>
