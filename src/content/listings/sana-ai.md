<h2>What Is Sana AI?</h2>
<p>Sana AI is an AI-powered learning and knowledge platform designed for enterprise teams. It combines adaptive learning, knowledge management, and AI-assisted content creation into a single platform that helps organizations onboard employees faster, share institutional knowledge, and deliver personalized training at scale. Sana AI uses artificial intelligence to understand what each employee needs to learn and delivers customized learning experiences.</p>
<h2>Key Features and Capabilities</h2>
<p>Sana AI provides a complete enterprise learning platform:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>AI course creation</strong> — Generate interactive training courses from existing content in minutes</li>
<li><strong>Adaptive learning</strong> — Personalize learning paths based on individual knowledge gaps</li>
<li><strong>Knowledge search</strong> — AI-powered search across all company knowledge resources</li>
<li><strong>Live sessions</strong> — Interactive live learning with AI-enhanced Q&#038;A and polls</li>
<li><strong>Analytics</strong> — Track learning progress, knowledge retention, and skill development</li>
<li><strong>Integrations</strong> — Connect with HR systems, Slack, Teams, and other enterprise tools</li>
</ul>
<h2>Pricing Plans</h2>
<p>Sana AI offers custom enterprise pricing based on the number of users and features required. A free trial is available for teams to evaluate the platform. Contact Sana for a demo and pricing specific to your organization&#8217;s learning and development needs.</p>
<h2>Who Should Use Sana AI?</h2>
<p>Sana AI is designed for enterprise learning and development teams. HR departments use it for employee onboarding and compliance training. Sales enablement teams deliver product knowledge training at scale. Customer success teams educate clients on product features. Remote organizations ensure consistent training across distributed teams. Fast-growing companies onboard new hires efficiently with standardized, AI-personalized learning paths.</p>
<h2>Sana AI vs Traditional LMS</h2>
<p>Traditional learning management systems require extensive manual course creation and deliver one-size-fits-all content. Sana AI automates course creation from existing materials and adapts learning paths to individual needs. Its AI-powered knowledge search turns your LMS into an intelligent assistant that answers questions in real time. For organizations serious about learning effectiveness, Sana AI represents a significant upgrade over traditional LMS platforms.</p>
