<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Is Dify?</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Dify is an open-source platform for building and operating AI applications, assistants, and workflows. It combines prompt management, datasets, tool integration, and app deployment into one product layer for teams that want to ship AI features faster.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key Features and Capabilities</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Dify provides a practical set of AI capabilities for modern users and teams:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>AI app platform</strong> – Build and manage assistants, workflows, and prompt-driven applications in one place</li>



<li><strong>Dataset and retrieval support</strong> – Connect knowledge sources for practical business use cases and internal tools</li>



<li><strong>Tool integration</strong> – Link models with external actions, APIs, and workflow steps</li>



<li><strong>Product-layer usability</strong> – More approachable for operations and product teams than lower-level frameworks</li>



<li><strong>Open-source deployment</strong> – Allows self-hosting for privacy, customization, and internal governance needs</li>



<li><strong>Team collaboration</strong> – Useful for organizations managing multiple AI projects under one system</li>
</ul>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Pricing Plans</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Dify is open-source for self-hosting, with additional hosted and enterprise options depending on deployment model. Costs vary based on infrastructure, team size, and connected model APIs.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Who Should Use Dify?</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Dify is a good fit for product teams, internal AI platform teams, and businesses that want to standardize how they create and manage AI applications. It is especially useful where both technical and non-technical stakeholders need to collaborate.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Dify vs Flowise vs AnythingLLM</h2>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Dify</strong>: Sits closer to the product layer, offering a more packaged environment for managing AI applications and workflows.</li>



<li><strong>Flowise</strong>: More visually workflow-centric.</li>



<li><strong>AnythingLLM</strong>: More focused on private knowledge workspaces.</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For teams building multiple AI products under one roof, Dify can be the most operationally convenient choice.</p>
